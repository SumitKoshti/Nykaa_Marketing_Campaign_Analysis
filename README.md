# Nykaa_Marketing_Campaign_Analysis
🎯 Project Objective: The objective of this project is to Analyze Nykaa’s marketing campaign data to evaluate campaign, channel, and audience performance, optimize marketing spend, identify key revenue and ROI drivers, and uncover funnel and seasonal trends using SQL, Python, and Power BI to support data-driven marketing decisions.

Key Objectives
- Analyze campaign performance across different marketing channels.
- Identify high-performing customer segments and campaigns.
- Measure key marketing KPIs such as Revenue, ROI, CTR, CPA, and Conversion Rate.
- Analyze customer funnel performance and seasonal trends.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to support data-driven decision-making.

📊 Insights & Recommendations -
1. Prioritize Email and SEO campaigns
Email delivered the highest ROI at 413.32% with the lowest acquisition cost of ₹68.2M, while SEO generated the highest revenue at ₹529.5M with a strong 346.29% ROI.
Recommendation: Increase or protect investment in Email for efficiency and SEO for revenue scale.

2. Reassess Influencer Marketing spend
Influencer campaigns spent ₹380.5M, the highest acquisition cost, but generated only ₹301.2M revenue and a -20.83% ROI.
Recommendation: Reduce or restructure influencer spending and evaluate individual influencers/campaigns before allocating additional budget.

3. Improve the Impression-to-Click stage
The funnel shows 1.97B impressions but only 75.46M clicks, resulting in a 3.82% CTR—the largest funnel drop-off.
Recommendation: Improve creatives, messaging, targeting, and CTAs to convert more impressions into clicks.

4. Use Email for conversion-focused campaigns
Email achieved the highest conversion rate of 16.78%, while SEO had the highest CTR at 6.50%.
Recommendation: Use SEO primarily to drive traffic and engagement, while using Email to nurture leads and maximize conversions.

5. Prioritize cost-efficient audiences
Working Professionals had the lowest overall CPA at ₹674.19, followed closely by Beauty Enthusiasts at ₹676.28.
Recommendation: Prioritize these audiences for acquisition campaigns while testing ways to reduce CPA for higher-cost segments.

6. Leverage festive periods for efficiency
Festive campaigns generated 72.08% ROI, compared with 62.56% for non-festive campaigns. However, non-festive campaigns generated higher overall revenue.
Recommendation: Increase strategic investment during festive periods while maintaining non-festive campaigns for revenue scale.

7. Investigate the 2025 revenue decline
Revenue was strong in the first half of 2025 but dropped sharply from approximately ₹113.7M in June to ₹28.6M in July and remained around ₹27–31M afterward.
Recommendation: Investigate campaign spend, channel performance, targeting, and data/business changes around June–July 2025 to identify the cause.

8. Optimize budget based on ROI, not revenue alone
SEO generated the highest revenue, but Email generated a substantially higher ROI. This shows that the highest-revenue campaign isn't necessarily the most efficient investment.
Recommendation: Allocate budgets using a combination of Revenue + ROI + CPA, rather than revenue alone.

9. Maintain a diversified audience strategy
Revenue is relatively evenly distributed across audiences, with each segment contributing around 19–20%.
Recommendation: Avoid over-dependence on a single audience and maintain diversified targeting while prioritizing segments with better CPA/ROI.
